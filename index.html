What is a lottery pool?

A lottery pool is a group participation system where multiple people contribute funds to play the lottery together as a team.

Pool Structure

✔ 10 fixed numbers
✔ $2 per number per draw

Transparency & Information

This is NOT a ticket sale or investment product.

It is a collaborative lottery pool where participants voluntarily contribute funds.
